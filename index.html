Privacy Policy for Big Fruit

Developer: Amal Thomas  |  Application: Big Fruit  |  Package: com.ziya.Fruit  |  Last Updated: October 31, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Amal Thomas ("we", "us", or "our") manages user information in relation to the mobile puzzle game Big Fruit ("the Game").

Our Privacy Commitment: Big Fruit is an offline casual game. We do not collect, store, or share any personal identifiable information from players.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Our game does not collect personal information of any kind. Specifically:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We never request or collect your name, email, phone number, address, or contacts.
  • No Accounts or Registration: You do not need to register, log in, or create an account to play.
  • No Location Tracking: We do not track or request GPS or geolocation data.
  • No Financial Information: We do not collect credit cards, payment details, or banking information.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: We do not access camera, microphone, device storage, or media libraries.

3. Local Storage on Device

To preserve your game progress, the game stores minimal, non-personal data strictly locally on your device:

  • High scores and unlocked levels.
  • Audio settings (sound effects and music on/off preferences).

This data stays on your device and is never transmitted over the internet. Deleting the app or clearing its data will reset this local progress.

4. Third-Party Services

The game is developed using the Unity engine (Unity Technologies). Unity may gather anonymous, technical hardware diagnostics (such as OS version, device model, and crash logs) strictly necessary for engine stability and graphics rendering.

For details on Unity's data practices, please visit the Unity Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

We take children's privacy seriously. Big Fruit is designed for all audiences, including families and children.

  • We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).
  • If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will delete it.

6. Security

Because the game does not collect or transmit personal information over the internet, your personal data is protected from external network breaches and unauthorized access.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect any changes in our practices or legal obligations. Updates will be published on this page with the revised date.
